## P0 Screens

### Today

Purpose: Present today's Climate Snapshot.

Layer tree:

- 00 Atmosphere
- 01 Header
- 02 Hero / Developing Halo
- 03 Insight
- 04 Suggestions
- 05 Navigation

Must exist:

- Atmosphere Layer
- Developing Halo
- Climate Snapshot
- Primary Statement
- Secondary Hint
- Suggestion Pills
- Minimal Navigation
- Read Entry
