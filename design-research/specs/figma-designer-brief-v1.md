## Non-Negotiable Rules

```text
Space before interface.
Air before information.
Development before presentation.
Photography before graphics.
One screen, one focus.
No dashboard.
No AI SaaS.
No spinner.
No nested cards.
No more than 3 visible suggestions on Today.
```
