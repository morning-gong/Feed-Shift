## Weekly Reflection

```text

01 Header
02 Weekly Summary
03 Pattern Reasons
04 Shift Direction
05 Return Context
```

Acceptance:

- Summary first.
- Pattern rows max 3.
- No 7-day chart.
- No journal letter layout.

## Monthly Reflection

Layer order:

```text
00 Atmosphere
01 Header
02 Monthly Summary
03 Pattern Shifts
04 Stable Signals
05 Next Month Direction
06 Return Context
```

Acceptance:

- No month calendar.
- No line chart.

```text
00 Atmosphere
01 Header
02 Annual Climate Portrait
03 Major Shifts
```

- No badges.

- No 12-month chart.

## Shared Reflection Rules

- Reflection screens are not reports.
- No achievement language.
- No gamification.
- No dense analytics.
- No raw history.
